# Asset inventory

The repository contains a complete local production asset set. No remote image hotlinks are used.

## Brand assets

- `public/assets/brand/logo-primary.svg` and `.png`: horizontal primary wordmark.
- `public/assets/brand/logo-mark.svg` and `.png`: compact brand mark.
- `public/favicon/favicon.svg` and `.png`: browser and manifest icons.
- `public/assets/og/og-home.jpg`: 1200 × 630 social sharing image.

## Fleet artwork

The truck images are original promotional artwork created for this site. They are K200- and K220-inspired visual treatments, not manufacturer photography and not a claim that the depicted vehicles are owned by 1st Class Express.

## Supporting artwork

- `public/assets/backgrounds/highway-dawn.webp`: shared interior-page backdrop.
- `public/assets/leadership/executive-profile.svg`: branded profile treatment used in place of an unavailable licensed portrait.

All photographic-style artwork is stored as WebP, dimensions are reserved in markup, the homepage hero is preloaded, and below-the-fold images use lazy loading.
