# Content source

## Source audit

The official square and portrait logo files, legacy fleet screenshot, archived production HTML and full-page legacy-site PDF supplied for the review were inspected alongside the current repository and live Vercel deployment. The deployed brand lockups are derived from the supplied official identity and remain the preferred web-ready assets.

Where the legacy archive and the current project brief differ, the current repository brief remains authoritative for contact details and leadership information. Legacy material is used for historical content and visual context, not to silently overwrite current business details.

## Preserved content

Company name, tagline, business statement, director name and title, phone number, email, LinkedIn URL, services, values, fleet categories, service areas, form requirements and disclosures are taken from the project brief.

## Rewritten content

Section copy was written for clarity and accessibility while preserving the stated service intent. Dangerous-goods wording and availability disclaimers are retained as supplied.

## Intentionally excluded

No physical address, awards, accreditations, years in business, fleet or capacity numbers, pricing, delivery guarantees, client logos, testimonials, partner logos or unsupported biographical details are included.
